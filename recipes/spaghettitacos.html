Spaghetti Taco

Description

my cousin spencer raves about these. honestly i tried them once and dont get the hype. My other cousin carly agrees these are kinda mid.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack of angel hair pasta
  • 1 jar of ragu
  • 12 hard taco shells
  • 1 cup of grated parmesan

Steps

  1. boil the pasta until it is tender but a little firm whne you bite it. drain
  2. return pasta to pot. add ragu and heat until warm
  3. spoon pasta into taco shells. top with parmesan
