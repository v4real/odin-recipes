Pretzel Pizza

Descrption

Soggy. Divorced Dad core. Brought to the attention of the masses by notable internet comedian Vic Michaelis, the pretzel pizza can be best described by two words: "An Experience".

Ingredients

  • 1 bag of hard pretzels
  • Ragu or marinara. sub ketchup if needed
  • shredded mozarella or a mexican cheese blend
  • peperoni (optional)

Steps

  1. pour pretzels onto a microwave safe plate
  2. drizzle whatever is your sauce base over the pretzels to preference.
  3. add cheese and pepperoni
  4. micro wave for like 3-5 mins until the cheese is completely melted and starts to bubble
