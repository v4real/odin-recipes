Ingredients

  • 1 Lb Italian Sausage
  • 3/4 lb lean ground beef
  • 2 cans canned tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp white sugar
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 12 Lasagna Noodles
  • 16 ounces of ricotta cheese
  • 5 lbs Mozerella shredded

Steps

  1. Gather ingredients
  2. cook meat over medium heat until browned
  3. Add the Tomato sauce sugar and italian seasoning. simmer covered for 2 hours
  4. boil and drain the noods
  5. preheat oven to 375 F
  6. in a 9x13 dish, put a layer of sauce, a layer of noodles, a layer of ricotta, half of the mozarella, the rest of the meat sauce, tthe rest of the noodles , then the left over mozarella
  7. bake for 25 minutes
